General questions

Developer

Who makes these apps?

All of them are built by Privatepappain, an independent Android developer focused on privacy-first, utility-first apps. You can see every app on our Google Play developer page.

Apps

Which apps do you offer?

Three: PrivateVault (free encrypted vault), PrivateVault PRO (paid, offline-first vault with optional encrypted cloud backup), and Habits Assistant (offline-first habit tracker).

Privacy

Do any of your apps sell my data?

No. None of our apps sell your data. Your personal content — vault files or habit records — stays on your device. Only advertising data (in the ad-supported apps) and optional Google sign-in/backup data leave the device, as described in each app's privacy policy.

Offline

Which apps work offline?

All are offline-first. PrivateVault PRO keeps your vault on-device; on its free plan it loads ads, and an optional subscription makes it ad-free. It otherwise uses the network only if you turn on the optional Cloud Vault, which backs up end-to-end-encrypted data to your own Google Drive. PrivateVault (Free) keeps the vault offline and uses the network only for ads. Habits Assistant stores your data on-device and uses the network only for optional sign-in/backup and ads.

Vault difference

What's the difference between PrivateVault and PrivateVault PRO?

Same encrypted vault, two editions. Free is ad-supported with the essentials and an image viewer; PRO is offline-first and adds in-app viewers for all file types, scan-to-PDF, folders, restore, and an optional end-to-end-encrypted Cloud Vault backup to your own Google Drive. PRO's features are available free with ads, or ad-free with an optional subscription. See the comparison on either app's page.

Support

How do I get support?

Email reply@privatepappa.in with your app name, app version, Android version, and device model. Don't send passwords or private files.

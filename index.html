One developer, one standard of privacy

Every Privatepappain app follows the same principles: collect as little as possible, keep your personal content on your device, and never sell your data. PrivateVault PRO is ad-free and offline-first, with an optional Cloud Vault that backs up only end-to-end-encrypted data to your own Google Drive; PrivateVault (Free) connects only to show ads; Habits Assistant keeps your habit records on-device and uses the network only for optional Google sign-in, cloud backup, and ads. You can read each app's own privacy policy, terms, and FAQ from its page.

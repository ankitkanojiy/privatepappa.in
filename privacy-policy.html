Privacy at Privatepappain

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Every app we publish follows the same principle: collect as little as possible and keep your personal content on your device. Each app has its own full privacy policy — this page summarizes them and links out.

Our shared principles

  • Your personal content — vault files or habit records — stays on your device.
  • We run no tracking servers and never sell your data.
  • Any data that leaves your device (ads, or optional Google sign-in/backup) is described plainly in the relevant app's policy.
  • We request only the permissions a feature needs.

PrivateVault (Free)

An offline encrypted file vault. Your vault contents are encrypted on-device and never transmitted. The app shows ads, so it uses network access only to deliver ads; the ad provider collects advertising identifiers and coarse IP-based location for that purpose.

Read the full PrivateVault privacy policy →

PrivateVault PRO

The paid edition is ad-free and offline-first. Your files are encrypted on-device and the developer never receives or can read them. It includes an optional Cloud Vault (off by default) that, only when you enable it, backs up end-to-end-encrypted data to your own Google Drive using the app's private Drive folder — keys never leave your device, so the developer cannot decrypt it.

Read the full PrivateVault PRO privacy policy →

Habits Assistant

An offline-first habit tracker. Your habits, streaks, and daily notes stay on-device. Optional Google sign-in enables encrypted cloud backup (basic profile and a user ID only — never Gmail, Drive, or Contacts). The app shows ads, with a consent dialog in the EEA/UK/Switzerland; advertising data is kept separate from your habit data.

Read the full Habits Assistant privacy policy →

Privacy note

We don't run cloud servers for your PrivateVault vaults, so we can't access vaults, reset vault passwords, or recover lost files. For Habits Assistant, optional cloud backup is tied to your own Google account. We never ask for your passwords.

Support guidance

When asking for support, include the app name, app version, Android version, device model, and a clear description of the issue. Please do not send vault passwords or private files.

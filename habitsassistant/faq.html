Frequently Asked Questions

Habits Assistant

Developer: Privatepappain • Habit tracker with an AI assistant
Offline

Does Habits Assistant work offline?

Yes. Habits, check-ins, streaks, notes, reminders, charts and the widget all work without an internet connection or an account, and your habits are stored on your phone. The app goes online only for the AI assistant, Google Drive backup, Google sign-in, subscriptions, and the rewarded ads you choose to watch.

Account

Do I need to sign in?

Not to track habits. Signing in with Google is needed only for Google Drive backup and for the AI features, because AI usage is counted per Google account.

Streaks

Can I mark past days?

You can mark or unmark today freely until midnight; after that the day is fixed. To catch up, you can mark earlier days this month (back to the day the habit was created). The app warns you first that any day you leave unmarked before then is recorded as missed for good. Missed days show as a red ring on the calendar.

Strictness

What is the difference between casual and strict habits?

You choose when you create a habit. For every habit, the name, emoji, colour and schedule are fixed once it is created. A casual habit's reminder and end date can still change. A strict habit is locked: only its end date can be extended. Deleting a strict habit asks for an extra confirmation.

Goals

What happens when a habit reaches its end date?

It moves to Accomplished goals with its success rate, how long it ran and its best streak. A goal that ends at 0% is labelled Failed. You can reopen a goal, retry a failed one, mark a goal accomplished early from its page, or delete it. On Pro and Plus the AI writes a short review of each goal; otherwise the review is built from your numbers on the phone.

Reminders

How do reminders work?

Set a reminder at a time of day or every few hours, with an optional note, and the app schedules it on your phone. You can mark the habit done from the notification. Reminders are rescheduled after a restart or a time-zone change. On Android 13 and later you're asked for notification permission. Battery optimisation, Do Not Disturb or a denied permission can delay or silence reminders, but tracking still works.

AI

What can the AI assistant do?

Ask it anything, or ask about your habits. It can build a habit from a sentence or suggest habits for a goal. It can also mark habits done or not done, but only for today, and set, change or turn off reminders. It does these straight away and lists every change in the chat as a summary you can't edit. If several habits have similar names, it asks which one you mean. New habits, pinning, end-date changes and deletions are shown as a card and only happen when you tap it. It follows the same locks you do. For example, it can't change a strict habit's reminder.

AI cost

Is the AI free?

On the free plan, each AI request is unlocked by watching short rewarded ads to the end. That is usually one ad, or a few more after heavy use in a month, and the app tells you how many before you start. An ad closed early unlocks nothing, though finished ads still count. Pro and Plus include a daily AI allowance with no ads; once it is used up, ads unlock more. AI needs a connected Google account and an internet connection.

AI data

What does the AI see?

Your message, the last few turns of the chat, and a summary of up to 12 habits: each habit's name, schedule, reminder time, progress this period, streak and recent completion rate. Your day-by-day check-in history, your notes, and your name and email are never sent. The privacy policy lists what each AI feature sends.

Ads

Does the app show ads?

Only rewarded ads, and only when you choose to watch one to unlock AI. There are no banner or pop-up ads, and tracking never shows an ad. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent message appears first, and you can change your choice later under Privacy choices in the app's menu.

Plans

What do Pro and Plus include?

A daily AI allowance with no ads to watch (Plus gives four times Pro's), deeper-thinking answers, smarter reminder lines, daily habit ideas, and AI reviews of finished goals. Both are billed monthly through Google Play. Cancel any time in the Play Store; your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Backup

How does Google Drive backup work?

Tap the Google icon at the top of the home screen (your profile picture, once signed in) to connect a Google account. Backups are saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Habits Assistant can open it, and the app can't see your other Drive files. A backup holds your habits, check-ins, notes, settings and AI chat, and is labelled with the phone's model, the number of habits and the time. Choose Auto-sync, daily, weekly, monthly or manual, or turn automatic backup off.

Restore

How do I restore after reinstalling or on a new phone?

Connect the same Google account and the app lists your backups. Pick one to restore. It becomes this phone's backup, so later changes are saved into it. If the phone already has habits, you choose to merge, replace them, or keep the phone's. If you start fresh instead, the app warns you that your newest backup will be overwritten and can't be recovered; your other backups are kept.

Backup

Can I delete a backup?

Yes. In the backup settings every backup has a delete button. Deleting this phone's own backup keeps all your habits on the phone and turns automatic backup off. You can also delete all of the app's backups on the web: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Habits Assistant → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Google data

What Google account data does the app use?

Your name, email address and profile picture from Sign in with Google, kept on your phone. If you use backup, the app also gets access to its own hidden folder in your Google Drive, and nothing else in your Drive. It never sees your password and does not access Gmail, Contacts or Calendar.

Charts

What do the progress charts show?

Each habit can be reviewed over 7 days, 1 month, 6 months or 1 year, with its completion rate and a comparison with the period before.

Widget

Is there a home-screen widget?

Yes. The "Today's habits" widget shows what is due today and lets you check habits off with a single tap. It updates as the day rolls over.

Notes

Can I add notes to a day?

Yes. Tap a date on a habit's calendar to add a note of up to 200 characters for that day.

Permissions

What permissions does it request?

Internet and network state (AI, backup, sign-in, ads), notifications and exact alarms (reminders), a short foreground service and start-up (to post and reschedule reminders), vibrate (haptics), and the advertising ID and Google Play billing. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS or storage access. The privacy policy has the full list.

Delete

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset all progress in the app's menu, Clear chat in the AI chat, and delete your backups in the backup settings, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Step-by-step instructions, including how to ask us by email, are on the Delete your data page.

Availability

Where can I download it?

Habits Assistant is for Android phones and is distributed through Google Play. The Get it on Google Play button on the app page opens its store listing.

Feature Availability Note

Some features depend on your device, Android version and settings. For example, battery optimisation and notification permissions affect reminders, and AI and backup need an internet connection and Google Play services. Not every feature behaves the same on every device.

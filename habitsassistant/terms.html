🔥 2. App Description

Habits Assistant is a habit tracker for Android, published by Privatepappain. It lets you create habits, check them off, build streaks, write short date notes, set reminders, review progress charts, track goals with an end date, and check habits off from a home-screen widget. It includes an optional AI assistant powered by Google's Gemini models, and optional backup to your own Google Drive. Your habits are stored on your device; the app goes online only for the AI assistant, backup, Google sign-in, subscriptions, and rewarded ads.

⭐ 3. Key Features

  • Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly habits with targets, emoji and colours
  • Calendar log with streaks, missed days and completion history
  • Casual and strict habits, end dates, and an Accomplished goals page
  • Daily notes (up to 200 characters per date)
  • Reminders at a set time or every few hours
  • Progress charts over 7 days, 1 month, 6 months and 1 year
  • Home-screen widget with one-tap check-off
  • AI assistant for questions, habit ideas, and changes to your habits
  • Optional Google Drive backup and restore
  • Free, with rewarded ads that unlock AI, and optional Pro and Plus subscriptions

🔑 4. Google Sign-in & Google Drive Backup

Signing in with Google is optional; every habit-tracking feature works without an account. Sign-in is needed for Google Drive backup and for the AI features. The app uses Google's Credential Manager to sign you in and never receives your Google password.

If you turn on backup, the app asks for access to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It can read and write only the files it creates there; it cannot see your other Drive files, and it does not access Gmail, Contacts or Calendar. Backups count toward your Google Drive storage and are kept until you delete them. Restoring a backup can merge with or replace the habits on your phone, and starting fresh overwrites your newest backup; the app explains each choice and asks you to confirm before anything is overwritten. You can sign out at any time, delete backups in the app or in Google Drive, and remove the app's access from your Google Account.

✨ 5. AI Assistant

The AI features send your requests to Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic, as set out in the Privacy Policy. They need a Google account and an internet connection.

  • Answers can be wrong. AI-generated text may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. The assistant is not a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial adviser, and its answers are not professional advice.
  • Changes to your habits. When you ask, the assistant can mark habits done or not done for today and set, change or turn off reminders. It applies these straight away and lists every change in the chat. New habits, other edits and deletions happen only when you tap to confirm. Please check what it has done; you can undo today's marks and reminder changes yourself.
  • Acceptable use. Do not use the AI to create content that is illegal, harmful, hateful, sexually explicit or deceptive, or otherwise in breach of Google's Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy. Do not try to make it reveal its instructions or work around its limits.
  • Reporting. If a reply is offensive or harmful, use Report a reply in the chat's menu, or email us.
  • Limits. AI is metered. On the free plan, requests are unlocked by rewarded ads; Pro and Plus include a daily allowance that resets each day and does not carry over. The size of the allowance and the number of ads needed for a request can change, for example when the cost of AI changes. The app always shows where you stand before you send a request.

📢 6. Rewarded Ads

Habits Assistant shows no banner or pop-up ads. Ads appear only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock an AI request. The ads are served by Google AdMob. A request is unlocked only after the ad confirms it was watched to the end. Unlocked requests have no cash value and cannot be transferred. For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads, and you can change your choice later under Privacy choices in the app's menu.

💳 7. Pro & Plus Subscriptions

  • Pro and Plus are optional monthly subscriptions sold through Google Play. The price is shown in your currency before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Google Play account. The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel it.
  • You can cancel at any time in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from the app's plan screen. After cancelling, your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Switching between Pro and Plus is handled by Google Play.
  • Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies.
  • Subscriptions change how AI is paid for. They do not unlock any habit-tracking feature, because all of those are free.

⚠️ 8. Limitations

Backup, sign-in, AI and ads rely on Google services and a working internet connection. They may be unavailable at times, and the app does not guarantee that they will always be available.

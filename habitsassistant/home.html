Habits Assistant

Habits Assistant helps you build daily routines and keep them going. Track habits, watch your streaks grow on a calendar, jot a short note on any day, set reminders, and review your progress over a week, month, six months, or a year. It is offline-first — your habit records live on your device — and optional Google sign-in adds encrypted cloud backup.

Features

A focused, modern habit tracker built with Material 3 — fast, tactile, and private by default.

🔥

Habits & streaks

Create habits with your own titles, colors and target days, then build a streak each time you check one off.

📅

Calendar log

See every habit on a month calendar. Long-press a date to mark it done, with subtle haptic feedback as you go.

📝

Daily notes

Attach a short note (up to 200 characters) to any date — a quick thought, a reason, or a reminder to yourself.

⏰

Reminders

Set daily reminder times for each habit. Alarms reschedule automatically after a reboot, and you stay in control of notifications.

📊

Progress charts

Review each habit as a clean chart over 7 days, 1 month, 6 months, or 1 year, with completion percentage and trend.

🧩

Home-screen widget

A “Today's habits” widget lets you check habits off straight from your home screen with a single tap.

📱

Offline-first

Your habits, history and notes are stored locally in the app's private database. The app works without an account.

☁️

Optional cloud backup

Sign in with Google to back up your habits so you can restore them on a new device. Sign-in is entirely optional.

✨

Modern & tactile

Material 3 design with smooth animations and haptics that make checking off a habit genuinely satisfying.

Private by default

Your habit records and daily notes stay on your device and are never sold or shared. The app uses the network only for optional Google sign-in and cloud backup, and to show ads that keep it free. Advertising data handled by Google AdMob is kept separate from your habit data. The full details are in the privacy policy.

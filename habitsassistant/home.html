Habits Assistant

Habits Assistant helps you build routines and keep them going. Track daily, weekly, monthly or yearly habits, watch your streaks grow on a calendar, set reminders, and review your progress. A built-in AI assistant can turn a sentence into a habit, answer your questions, and mark habits done for you. Your habits are stored on your phone, and you can back them up to your own Google Drive.

Features

A focused habit tracker built with Material 3: quick to use, honest about your progress, and private by default.

🔥

Habits & streaks

Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly habits, each with a target per period, your own emoji and colour, and a streak that grows every time you keep it.

📅

Calendar log

Every habit on a month calendar. Mark or unmark today until midnight, catch up on earlier days this month after a clear warning, and see missed days as a red ring.

🎯

Casual or strict

Choose how firm each habit is. A casual habit's reminder and end date can change later; a strict habit is locked, and its end date can only be pushed later.

🏁

Goals with an end date

Give a habit a "run until" date. When it ends it moves to Accomplished goals with its success rate and a short review. Reopen it or try again any time.

⏰

Reminders

Remind yourself at a set time or every few hours, with your own note. Mark a habit done straight from the notification. Reminders come back after a restart.

✨

AI assistant

Ask anything, describe a habit in your own words, or ask it to mark habits done for today or change a reminder. Every change it makes is listed in the chat.

📊

Progress charts

Review each habit over 7 days, 1 month, 6 months or 1 year, with its completion rate and how it compares with the period before.

🧩

Home-screen widget

The "Today's habits" widget lets you check habits off with a single tap, right from your home screen.

📝

Daily notes

Add a short note, up to 200 characters, to any date: a quick thought, a reason, or something to remember.

☁️

Google Drive backup

Optional. Backups go to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Restore one after reinstalling or on a new phone, choose how often it runs, and delete backups any time.

📱

Works offline

Tracking, notes, reminders, charts and the widget work without an internet connection or an account. Only AI and backup need to go online.

🎉

Satisfying to use

An emoji burst when you check a habit off, subtle haptics, smooth animations, and an Auto, Light or Dark theme.

Free, Pro and Plus

Every tracking feature is free, with no banner or pop-up ads. Plans only change how you pay for AI.

Free

  • Every habit, reminder, chart and widget feature
  • Backup to your own Google Drive
  • No ads while you track. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one to unlock AI
  • Each AI request is unlocked by short rewarded ads watched to the end. The app shows how many before you start

Pro & Plus

  • Everything in Free
  • A daily AI allowance with no ads to watch. Plus gives four times Pro's
  • Deeper-thinking AI answers, deepest on Plus
  • Smarter reminder lines, daily habit ideas and AI goal reviews
  • Used up the day's AI? Short ads unlock more
  • Billed monthly through Google Play. Cancel any time

Private by default

Your habits, check-ins, notes and AI chat are stored on your phone and are never sold. The app goes online only for the features you use: the AI assistant (through Google's Gemini), backup to your own Google Drive, Google sign-in, subscriptions through Google Play, and the rewarded ads you choose to watch. The full details are in the privacy policy.

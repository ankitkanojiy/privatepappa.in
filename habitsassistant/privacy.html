🔥 1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Habits Assistant is developed and published by Privatepappain, an independent Android app developer dedicated to privacy-conscious, utility-first mobile experiences. Habits Assistant is designed to help you organize daily routines, track completion streaks, write date-specific notes, and maintain positive habits.

We believe that your personal growth and habit records belong to you alone. This privacy policy clearly and transparently explains:

  • Why your habit tracking records and daily notes stay strictly on your local device.
  • Exactly which Google Account details are processed when you choose to sign in, and why they are used solely for account authentication and the optional on-device backup.
  • Precisely what data the ad service collects to serve ads, and how that advertising telemetry is kept strictly separate from your personal habits.
  • How you maintain 100% control over your data, permissions, and privacy choices.

Key Principle: Privatepappain does not operate tracking servers or sell user data. Your habit records, notes, and personal routines are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party data brokers or marketing aggregators.

📱 2. On-Device Habit Data & Daily Notes (Offline-First)

At its core, Habits Assistant is architected as an offline-first application. All your habit information is saved directly on your device, in a data file kept inside the app's own private storage area, which Android isolates from every other installed app.

This on-device information includes:

  • Habit Details: Habit titles, descriptions, color themes, icon indicators, and target frequencies (e.g., daily, specific days of the week).
  • Completion Histories: Check-in dates, historical logs, completion timestamps, and calculated streak counters.
  • Daily Date Notes: Notes up to 200 characters associated with specific calendar dates (accessible via single-tap on any calendar date).
  • Reminder Settings: User-configured daily alarm times and reminder notification preferences.

Device Isolation: This personal habit data is never transmitted to any Privatepappain server. It resides inside Android's secure application sandbox and cannot be accessed by other installed apps.

🔑 3. Google Account Login & Authentication Data

Habits Assistant offers an optional Sign in with Google feature powered by Google's modern Android Credential Manager API (OAuth 2.0 Client ID: 308567815578-ft0ociv79ul48ct8qbj4m5vf7t7vljq9.apps.googleusercontent.com). Signing in is entirely optional; all habit-tracking features work without an account.

When you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides verified profile credentials through official Google Play Services sheets. We collect and process only the minimal data necessary to enable your login session:

Google Account Data Field How It Is Processed Specific Purpose Shared With 3rd Parties?
Email Address Received from Google Credential Manager token; stored locally in secure app preferences. Acts as your unique account identifier, so each account's backup is kept separate and can be offered back to you when you sign in again. Never Shared
Display Name Received from your Google profile; saved in local app preferences. Displayed in the user account dialog to personalize your greeting (e.g., "Welcome back, John"). Never Shared

Google User ID & ID Token Cryptographic JSON Web Token (JWT) issued by Google. Authenticates that you are the legitimate owner of the Google Account without the app ever seeing or storing your Google password. Never Shared

How Account Backups Work When Signed In:

When you are signed in, Habits Assistant keeps a snapshot of your habit configurations, streaks and date notes, filed under your sanitized Google User ID so that each account's backup stays separate. This snapshot is stored in the app's private storage on your device; your habits, check-ins and notes are never uploaded to Google or to us (the one thing that does leave your device is described in section 5, and it is never your habit data) — it is what lets you recover your habits after signing out and back in, or after resetting your habit list, and it does not travel to another phone. You choose how often it is refreshed (as changes happen, daily, weekly, monthly, or only when you ask), and whenever a backup already exists the app asks whether to merge it with what is on the device, replace the device's habits with it, or keep the device's habits.

Strict Privacy Guarantees for Google Account Data:

  • No Sensitive Scopes: Habits Assistant does NOT request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or YouTube data. We only request basic Sign-in profile and email scopes.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: Your Google identity is never linked to advertising profiles or shared with advertising networks.
  • No Passwords Handled: Authentication is handled by Google's native system dialogs; Habits Assistant never has access to your password.
  • Easy Disconnect: You can sign out at any time from the in-app account dialog, or revoke the app's permissions at any time from your Google Account Security Dashboard (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

To deliver ads, prevent fraudulent clicks, enforce frequency capping, and comply with legal requirements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain technical telemetry. This data is collected for advertising and security purposes only:

Ad Data Collected Collected By Exact Advertising Purpose User Control
Mobile Advertising Identifier (GAID / AAID) Google Mobile Ads SDK Enables ad serving, frequency capping (preventing the same ad from showing repeatedly), and ad attribution. You can reset or completely delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Separation of Advertising and Personal Habit Data: The ad service operates independently of your habit tracking records. It cannot read your habit names, streaks, calendar completions, date notes, or personal routines. Your personal data is never shared with advertisers.

GDPR / European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Swiss Consent:

✨ 5. AI Features & What They Send

Habits Assistant has optional features that use Google's Gemini models. Most of them turn something you write into a habit suggestion. One of them, the Assistant, is an open-ended chat: you can ask it about anything, and it can also build a habit for you from a description. These features are the only part of the app that sends anything off your device. Most run only when you use them; three of them — the habit ideas on your home screen, the reminder nudges and the daily encouragement line — run on their own, at most once a day each, and the table below says exactly what each one sends. None of them sends your check-in calendar or your notes. The Assistant is additionally told how your habits are set up and how far along you are in the current period, because that is what lets it answer questions about them and change their settings when you ask it to.

Exactly what is sent, and when:

Feature What Leaves Your Device When
Assistant chat Whatever you type into the chat, on any subject, plus the recent messages in that conversation so replies follow on, and a summary of the habits you already track: each habit's name, how often it repeats and its target, what time it reminds you, how much of the current period is already done, and the current streak. That summary is what lets it answer "have I finished today?" and change a habit's settings when you ask. Not sent: your check-in dates, your calendar history, your date notes or your reminder notes. Each time you send a message.
Describe a habit in words Only the sentence you type into that field, for example "save from salary every 1st". When you tap to fill the form.
Start from a goal Only the goal you type, for example "get fit before summer". When you tap to get suggestions.
Habit ideas on the home screen The names of habits you already track, so suggestions don't repeat them, and your own recent chat messages so the ideas follow what you have been talking about. No check-in dates, no streaks, no notes. Once a day, on its own, and again if you tap to refresh the ideas. The card itself is always on screen, but the ideas behind it are only fetched once a day.
Reminder nudges For habits that have a reminder but no note of your own: the habit's name and how often it repeats, so a line can be written for its notification. Nothing for habits where you wrote your own note — yours is used instead. No check-in dates, no streaks. Once a day, on its own. The lines are written ahead of time and stored on your device, because a reminder has to appear instantly even with no connection.
Daily encouragement line A short count-only summary, for example "2 of 5 habits done today, best current streak 12 periods". No habit names, no dates, no notes. At most once a day, and only while you have habits.

Where your conversation is kept: the chat is saved on your device, in the app's private storage, under the Google Account you are signed in to — so signing out and back in brings it back, and two accounts on one phone never see each other's. It is not uploaded to Google or to us: like your habit backup, it lives only on the device, and it travels in that backup so a restore brings it back with your habits. You can erase it at any time with Clear chat in the chat's own menu, and clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes it along with everything else.

What is never sent:

  • Your check-in dates and calendar logs — the day-by-day record of what you did and when never leaves the device. The Assistant chat is told how each habit is set up, this period's progress and the current streak, exactly as the table above sets out; the other AI features send only habit names, or counts.
  • Your daily date notes or reminder notes.
  • Your account backup, which stays in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your name, email address, Google ID or profile picture. AI requests are not tied to your identity.

Who processes it: requests go to Google through Firebase AI Logic, which passes them to the Gemini API. Google processes them as a data processor for us under the Firebase data processing terms, and their handling of the request is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not keep a copy of what you type, and we do not use it to train anything.

Suggestions are suggestions: an AI suggestion only fills in a form, offers a tile or shows a habit card. Nothing is saved to your habits until you tap to add it, and you can edit every detail first. A change the Assistant offers to make to a habit you already keep works the same way: it appears as a card spelling out what the habit would look like afterwards, and nothing is altered until you tap Apply change. Deleting a habit is not something the Assistant can do at all. Generated text can be wrong, and the Assistant is not a doctor, lawyer or financial adviser — please do not treat it as one. If a reply or a suggestion is offensive or harmful, the Report option beside it, or in the chat's menu, opens an email to reply@privatepappa.in so we can look into it.

Turning it off: these features are only ever triggered by you, so simply not using them means nothing is sent. They also require a network connection and are unavailable offline, in which case the app falls back to its own built-in habit templates and encouragement. There is a limit on how many AI requests the app will make in a day; once it is reached, the AI features stop until the following day and the rest of the app carries on as normal.

⚙️ 6. Android System Permissions & Purpose

Habits Assistant adheres to Android's principle of least privilege. We request only the minimal permissions necessary for core app features:

Permission Name Android Level Exact Technical Function
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required to communicate with Google servers for Sign in with Google, loading your Google profile picture, and loading ads. Backups need no network - they are written on the device.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Allows the app to check whether an active Wi-Fi or mobile data connection exists before attempting network operations.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Requested only if you choose to enable daily habit reminder alarms. If denied, reminders are silenced but habit tracking continues to work normally.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal Allows the app's broadcast receiver to reschedule your configured local alarms after your phone is restarted (since Android clears pending alarms on reboot).
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback when you hold or long-press a calendar date to mark a habit as completed.

Permissions We Explicitly DO NOT Request:

Habits Assistant does not request or access:

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Precise (GPS) or Background Location permissions.
  • No Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS permissions.
  • No broad External Storage or media read/write permissions.

🗑️ 8. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You have complete ownership over your data at all times:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete any individual habit, check-in, or date note directly within the app. Clearing app storage in Android Settings → Apps → Habits Assistant → Storage → Clear Data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored habits and notes from your device.
  • Google Account Logout: Tapping your profile avatar and selecting "Sign Out" removes all cached profile tokens and account identifiers from your device immediately.
  • Backup Deletion: The account backup lives in the app's private storage on your device, so it is yours to remove: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it along with everything else. Because we never receive a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request you need to send us.

🛡️ 9. Security Safeguards

We implement rigorous industry-standard security safeguards to protect your information:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): All network communications with Google authentication and ad servers are strictly encrypted in transit using HTTPS and modern TLS protocols.
  • Application Sandboxing: Android's Linux kernel-level sandbox isolates Habits Assistant's internal database and preferences, preventing unauthorized access by other apps.
  • No Password Storage: Sign in with Google utilizes cryptographic tokens; your Google Account password is never accessed, handled, or stored by our app.
  • Safe Credential Storage: Account tokens and preferences are stored using Android's private MODE_PRIVATE storage.

👶 10. Children's Privacy

Habits Assistant is a general audience productivity tool and is not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us at reply@privatepappa.in, and we will promptly delete such information.

🔥 1. Overview

Habits Assistant is a habit tracker for Android, developed and published by Privatepappain, an independent app developer. It helps you build routines, track streaks, write short date notes, set reminders, and get help from an AI assistant.

This policy explains:

  • What the app keeps on your phone.
  • What leaves your phone, and only when you use the feature that needs it: Google sign-in, Google Drive backup, the AI assistant, subscriptions, and rewarded ads.
  • Which Google services process that data, and how to delete it.

Key principle: Privatepappain does not run servers for this app and has no database of users. We never receive your habits, notes, chat or account details, and we never sell or rent your data.

📱 2. Data Kept on Your Phone

Everything you create in the app is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from every other app. This includes:

  • Habits: names, emoji, colours, schedules and targets, casual or strict setting, end dates, pinned and accomplished status.
  • Check-ins: the days you completed each habit, from which streaks, missed days and charts are worked out.
  • Date notes: notes of up to 200 characters attached to calendar dates.
  • Reminders: reminder times or intervals and any reminder notes you write.
  • AI chat history: your conversation with the assistant, kept separately for each Google account you sign in with.
  • AI preferences: a short list of topics you often bring up and habit ideas you turned down, so suggestions don't repeat. Also saved here: AI-written reminder and encouragement lines, kept so they appear instantly offline.
  • Settings: theme, backup preferences, your plan, and the signed-in account's name, email and profile picture link.

Not in Android's own backup: the app excludes all of its data from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. The only copy that leaves your phone is the Google Drive backup you choose to turn on (section 4).

☁️ 4. Google Drive Backup

Backup is optional. When you turn it on, the app asks Google for access to a hidden app data folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Only Habits Assistant can open that folder. The app can't see anything else in your Drive, and the folder doesn't appear in your Drive file list.

What a backup contains:

  • Your habits, check-ins, date notes and reminder settings.
  • Your AI chat history, so a restore brings it back too.
  • A label so you can tell backups apart: your phone's maker and model, the number of habits, and when it was saved.

Your name and email are deliberately left out of the backup file, since it already sits in your own account.

How it works:

  • You choose when it runs: shortly after each change (Auto-sync), daily, weekly, monthly, or only when you tap Back up now. Automatic backup can be switched off at any time.
  • After reinstalling or on a new phone, connecting the same account lists your backups so you can pick one to restore. The restored backup then keeps receiving this phone's changes.
  • Choosing to start fresh instead overwrites your newest backup; the app warns you first that it can't be recovered. Your other backups are kept.
  • Backups are sent over HTTPS directly between your phone and Google Drive. They never pass through us. The app doesn't add its own encryption; backups are protected by your Google account and stored by Google Drive with Google's encryption at rest.
  • Data from Google Drive is used only to back up and restore your habits. It is not used for ads, not shared with anyone, and not read by people.

Deleting backups: each backup in the app's backup settings has a delete button. Deleting the current phone's backup keeps your habits on the phone and turns automatic backup off. You can also remove all of the app's backups from Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Habits Assistant → Options → Delete hidden app data. See Delete your data.

✨ 5. AI Features & What They Send

Habits Assistant's AI features use Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. They need a connected Google account and an internet connection. On the free plan, an AI request runs only after you choose to watch rewarded ads for it; on Pro and Plus it uses your daily allowance first. Three features run on their own, and only on Pro and Plus: reminder lines, encouragement lines and goal reviews. Habit ideas also refresh once a day on those plans. On the free plan, nothing is sent unless you ask for it. The table below lists exactly what each feature sends.

Feature What Leaves Your Phone When
Assistant chat Your message (on any subject), a shortened copy of the last few turns of the chat, the short topic list and declined ideas from section 2, and a summary of up to 12 habits: each habit's name, schedule, reminder time, progress this period, current streak, recent completion rate, casual or strict setting, end date, and whether it is pinned. Each time you send a message.
Describe a habit in words Only the sentence you type, for example "stretch every morning at 7". When you tap to fill the form.
Start from a goal The goal you type, and ideas you turned down before. When you tap to get suggestions.
Habit ideas The names and schedules of up to 12 habits with their recent completion rate, a few of your recent chat messages (shortened), your topic list, and ideas you turned down or were shown recently. When you ask for ideas. On Pro and Plus, also once a day on its own.
Reminder lines (Pro and Plus) The name and schedule of each habit that has a reminder but no note of your own, so a line can be written for its notification. Once a day, on its own.
Encouragement lines (Pro and Plus) Counts only, for example "3 of 5 habits done today, best streak 12 days". No habit names. At most every six hours, while you have habits.
Goal reviews (Pro and Plus) A finished goal's name and its totals: how long it ran, number of check-ins, best streak and success rate. When a goal is finished and its review is written.

Never sent to the AI:

  • Your day-by-day check-in history and calendar.
  • Your date notes and reminder notes.
  • Your name, email address or profile picture. AI requests are not labelled with your identity.

What the assistant can change: when you ask, it can mark habits done or not done for today only, and set, change or turn off reminders. It applies these straight away and lists every change in the chat. New habits, other edits and deletions appear as a card and happen only when you tap it. It follows the app's locks, so it can't change a strict habit's reminder or any habit's name, emoji, colour or schedule.

Who processes it: requests go over HTTPS to Google through Firebase AI Logic, which passes them to the Gemini API. They do not pass through us, and we do not keep a copy. Google handles them under the Firebase terms, the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may keep requests for a limited time to detect abuse. Where the service runs on Google's unpaid tier, Google's terms also allow it to use requests to improve its products. Please don't put sensitive personal information in AI messages.

AI can be wrong: generated text may be inaccurate, and the assistant is not a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial adviser. If a reply is offensive or harmful, use Report a reply in the chat's menu, which opens an email to reply@privatepappa.in.

Where the chat is kept: on your phone, separately for each signed-in Google account, and in your Google Drive backup if backup is on. Erase it at any time with Clear chat in the chat's menu.

📢 6. Rewarded Ads & Advertising Data

Habits Assistant shows no banner or pop-up ads. The only ads are rewarded video ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC), which play only when you choose to watch one to unlock an AI request. The Google Mobile Ads SDK runs in the app to load and show them, and collects the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

Ad Data Collected Collected By Purpose Your Control
Advertising ID Google Mobile Ads SDK Serving ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, and measuring ads. Reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by phone).
IP address & approximate location Google Mobile Ads SDK A coarse region inferred from your IP address, for local ad rules and fraud detection. The app never requests GPS or precise location.
Device information Google Mobile Ads SDK Device model, Android version, screen size and network type, so ads display correctly. Collected only by the ads SDK.
Ad interactions & diagnostics Google Mobile Ads SDK Impressions, taps, video completion, load times and ad-related crashes, to detect fraud and bill advertisers. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one.

An ad unlocks AI only once it has played to the end.

Kept apart from your habits: the ads SDK cannot read your habits, check-ins, notes or chat, and none of them are shared with advertisers. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform. Where required, a consent message appears before any ad is requested, letting you choose personalised or non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under Privacy choices in the app's menu.

💳 7. Subscriptions & Purchases

Pro and Plus are optional monthly subscriptions sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment, so we never receive your card or payment details. When the app opens, it asks Google Play which plan is active and when it renews or ends, and keeps that on your phone to set your daily AI allowance. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

🧮 8. AI Usage Record & Security Checks

AI usage record: every AI request has a real cost, so the app keeps a small record of how much AI each Google account uses. It uses the record to apply plan allowances and ad unlocks fairly. The record holds only counts, such as AI used today and this month and ads watched this month. It never includes your habits, notes, messages or email, it is not sent to us, and it is used for nothing else.

Security checks: to protect the AI service from misuse, Google's security services check that AI requests come from a genuine copy of the app. These checks send technical app and device information to Google, which uses it only for security under its own terms.

🗑️ 11. Data Retention & Deletion

  • On your phone: kept until you delete it. Delete habits one by one, use Reset all progress in the menu, Clear chat in the AI chat, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything.
  • Google Drive backups: kept in your Drive until you delete them in the app's backup settings or in Google Drive's Manage apps. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete them, so you can restore them later.
  • AI requests: not stored by us. Google keeps them only as its terms allow (section 5).
  • AI usage record: kept to apply AI limits fairly. It holds only counts, never your habits or messages.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time.
  • Google account access: remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete your data page. You can also email reply@privatepappa.in with the subject "Delete my Habits Assistant data". We will reply within 30 days and help you remove anything held by the services above.

🛡️ 12. Security

  • Encrypted in transit: everything the app sends (sign-in, Drive backup, AI requests, subscriptions, ads) goes over HTTPS/TLS.
  • App sandbox: Android isolates the app's private storage from other apps.
  • No passwords: Google sign-in uses tokens; the app never handles your Google password.
  • Short-lived access: Drive access uses short-lived tokens from Google Play services, and the app can reach only its own backup folder.
  • Protected components: the app's reminder and backup receivers are not open to other apps, so no other app can mark your habits done or trigger a backup.

👶 13. Children's Privacy

Habits Assistant is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the app in a way that shared their information, contact us at reply@privatepappa.in and we will help remove it.

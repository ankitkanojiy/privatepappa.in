🔍 1. Where your data is

Habits Assistant does not run its own servers and has no account database. Your data can only be in these places, and you can delete it from each of them yourself:

  • On your phone - your habits, check-ins, notes, reminders, AI chat history and settings.
  • In your own Google Drive - backups, only if you connected a Google account and backup is on. They sit in a private app folder that only Habits Assistant can open.
  • With Google services the app uses - AI requests (Gemini, through Firebase), rewarded ads (AdMob), subscriptions (Google Play) and a small record of your AI usage.

📱 2. Delete data on your phone

  • Habits, check-ins and notes: on the home screen, open the ⋮ menu and choose Reset all progress, then confirm.
  • AI chat history: open the AI Assistant, tap its ⋮ menu and choose Clear chat.
  • Everything at once: go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Habits Assistant → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Deleting data on the phone does not delete your Google Drive backups. Delete those as described below if you want them gone too.

☁️ 3. Delete your Google Drive backups

In the app: tap your profile picture at the top right to open the backup settings. Under Backups in your Google Drive, tap the delete icon next to a backup and confirm. Each backup is deleted from your Drive for good.

Deleting the backup of the phone you are using keeps every habit on that phone and turns automatic backup off, so nothing is uploaded again until you turn it back on or tap Back up now.

From Google Drive on the web (deletes all of the app's backups, even without the app):

  1. Open drive.google.com and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Click the Settings (gear) icon → Settings → Manage apps.
  3. Find Habits Assistant, click Options → Delete hidden app data.
  4. Optionally, choose Options → Disconnect from Drive as well.

🔑 4. Disconnect your Google account

  • In the app: open the backup settings and choose Logout from Google Account. Your habits stay on the phone.
  • From your Google Account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, select Habits Assistant and remove its access. This withdraws its access to your Google Drive backup folder.

✨ 5. AI chat and the AI usage record

When you use an AI feature, what that feature needs - such as your message, recent chat and habit names - is sent to Google's Gemini through Firebase AI Logic to produce the answer. We do not keep a copy on any server of ours. Your chat history is kept on your phone and in your Drive backups; delete it with Clear chat and by deleting the backups (sections 2 and 3).

The app also keeps a small record of how much AI you have used, so that plan allowances and ad unlocks are applied fairly. It holds only counts - AI used and ads watched this month - never your habits, notes or messages, and it is not used for anything else.

📢 6. Advertising data

Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one to unlock AI. They are served by Google AdMob, which processes data such as your IP address, ad interactions and advertising ID as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, change your ad consent in the app's ⋮ menu → Privacy choices.

💳 7. Subscriptions

Pro and Plus subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies; you can cancel a subscription in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

✉️ 8. Ask us to delete your data

You can also email us. Write to the address below with the subject "Delete my Habits Assistant data" and the Google account email you used in the app. We will reply within 30 days, confirm what was deleted, and help you remove anything held by the services listed above.

reply@privatepappa.in

🗂️ 9. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted, by you, right away: habits, check-ins, notes and chat on your phone; your Google Drive backups; the app's access to your Google account.
  • Kept: the AI usage record (counts only, used to apply AI limits); purchase records kept by Google Play; and data held by Google under its own policies for AI requests and ads, which you can manage from your Google Account.

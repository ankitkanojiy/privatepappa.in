PrivateVault

PrivateVault is a free, offline encrypted file vault. Import your private photos, videos and documents, encrypt them locally with AES-256-GCM, and unlock them with a password or your fingerprint. Your files stay on your device — the vault works offline, and the app connects to the network only to show ads that keep it free.

What PrivateVault does

Real on-device encryption — not hiding or renaming — for the files you choose to protect.

🔐

AES-256-GCM vault

Files you import are encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2 key derivation. The vault works fully offline.

🖼️

Secure image viewer

View, zoom and pan your images inside the vault. They are decrypted only in memory for display and never written outside the vault.

👆

Password & biometric unlock

Unlock with a vault password (up to 127 characters) or your device biometrics. Auto-lock protects the vault when you step away.

📥

Import & export on your terms

Import via Android's document picker (no broad storage permission). Export decrypted copies only when you explicitly choose to.

💾

Encrypted backups

Create password-protected encrypted backups to keep a copy safe — or to move your vault into PrivateVault PRO later.

🛡️

Screenshot protection

Sensitive vault screens block screenshots and screen recording in the release build. Share or open-with only when you act.

How to move to PRO

In PrivateVault, create a password-protected encrypted backup and save it anywhere on your device. Open PrivateVault PRO, choose Restore, pick that backup file, and enter the same backup password. The move itself is fully local and encrypted — no cloud transfer required. In PRO you can optionally enable the end-to-end-encrypted Cloud Vault afterward.

App Description & Architecture

PrivateVault PRO is an offline-first encrypted vault app for Android, published by Privatepappain. It helps you store, view, and manage personal files securely on your device. All encryption and storage operate locally using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with Argon2id v1.3 key derivation, and your encryption keys never leave your device.

The app does not require any account to use its core features. An optional Cloud Vault lets you connect your own Google account to back up end-to-end-encrypted vault data to your own Google Drive; it is off by default and described below.

Internet access is requested to load ads on the Free plan, verify Google Play subscriptions, and — only if you enable Cloud Vault — synchronize your encrypted backups with your Google Drive. The vault engine encrypts your data on-device before anything is uploaded.

Key Features

  • Offline-first encrypted file storage (AES-256-GCM, Argon2id v1.3) with on-device-only keys
  • Optional end-to-end-encrypted Cloud Vault backup to your own Google Drive (off by default)
  • Free plan with ad support and full vault functionality
  • Optional Pro annual subscription for a completely ad-free, instant-unlock experience
  • In-app viewers for images, videos, audio, PDFs, documents, archives, text, and code
  • Scan-to-PDF, encrypted notes, and local file organization
  • Streamed encrypted backup creation and verification
  • Panic Lock, biometric authentication, and screenshot prevention

Cloud Vault & Google Account (Optional)

Cloud Vault is an optional feature that is disabled by default. You may enable it to back up your vault to your own Google Drive so you can restore it on another device. By enabling it, you agree to the following:

  • You connect your own Google account, and you grant the app access only to its private Drive Application Data Folder (the drive.appdata scope). The app cannot access any other file in your Drive.
  • Only end-to-end-encrypted vault data is uploaded. Encryption keys are derived from your vault password and recovery key and never leave your device. The developer cannot decrypt, read, or recover your Cloud Vault data, and cannot reset your password or restore your backup for you.
  • Cloud backups are stored in, and count against, your Google Drive storage. You are responsible for maintaining access to your Google account.
  • You can disable Cloud Vault, delete your cloud backups, and unlink your Google account from within the app at any time. Use of Google services is also governed by Google's own terms.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Plans & Subscriptions

PrivateVault PRO offers two access tiers:

  • Free Plan: Provides access to all vault features supported by advertisements. Internet connectivity is required to load verified ads for vault access.
  • Pro Subscription (Ad-Free): An optional yearly subscription ("Ad-Free Plan") purchased through Google Play In-App Billing that permanently removes all advertisements and ad network requests for an instant, uninterrupted unlock experience.

All subscription fees, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are managed directly by Google Play under Google's Terms of Service. Subscriptions automatically renew on an annual basis unless cancelled prior to the end of the current billing cycle through your Google Play Store account settings.

Advertisements on Free Plan

On the Free plan, PrivateVault PRO displays advertisements. To access the vault for free, an active internet connection is required to load verified ads. Users can choose between personalized and non-personalized ads via the built-in "Interest-based ads" toggle in Settings > General.

Attempting to bypass, alter, or manipulate ad delivery mechanisms or network reachability checks on the Free plan is prohibited and may prevent access to vault features until an ad is legitimately verified or an Ad-Free subscription is active.

Limitations

PrivateVault PRO cannot guarantee that every file type will preview or render perfectly. Unsupported, corrupted, extremely large, unusual, or password-protected files may fail to open, preview, or process. Scan-related features depend on image quality, lighting, document angle, and supported native components.

Screenshot protection and app obfuscation reduce risk but cannot provide absolute protection against rooted devices, compromised systems, external cameras, screen mirroring bypasses, malware, or advanced reverse engineering.

PrivateVault PRO does not provide cloud recovery, server-side password reset, or developer recovery access. If a vault password and recovery code are lost, encrypted files cannot be recovered by the developer.

Encryption itself does not damage files. The real risk is an interrupted or incomplete write — caused by the phone powering off, storage running out, the app being force-closed, or cleaner apps modifying vault data. PrivateVault PRO uses authenticated encryption, which means even one altered byte will cause the app to block access to that file as a safety feature.

What PrivateVault PRO is for

PrivateVault PRO is a personal file-security app for Android. Its purpose is simple: let you keep your own photos, videos, documents, audio, archives, and notes in a private, password-protected vault that is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. You import files into the vault, view and manage them inside the app, and only you — with your password and recovery code — can decrypt them.

How Google services are used. The app works fully on your device and needs no account to use its core features. One optional feature, Cloud Vault, lets you sign in with your own Google account and back up your vault to a private folder in your own Google Drive (the Drive drive.appdata scope), so you can restore it on a new device. Only data that is already end-to-end encrypted on your device is uploaded; the app cannot see any other file in your Drive, and the developer can never read your backups. This is the only Google user-data access the app requests.

Google API compliance. PrivateVault PRO's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app is a private file vault only. It does not generate, process, request, or facilitate any AI-generated content, and it is not used to create non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) or any other prohibited content. Bringing your vault from Free

Already using PrivateVault? In the free app, create a password-protected encrypted backup and save it to your device. Then in PrivateVault PRO choose Restore, select that backup, and enter the same backup password. Everything stays encrypted end to end — and if you later enable the optional Cloud Vault, only that already-encrypted data is backed up to your own Google Drive.

PrivateVault PRO

PrivateVault PRO is the paid upgrade to PrivateVault: the same AES-256-GCM encrypted vault, but completely ad-free and with no network permission at all. Nothing it stores can leave your device over the internet, because it cannot reach the internet. Bringing your vault from Free

Already using PrivateVault? In the free app, create a password-protected encrypted backup and save it to your device. Then in PrivateVault PRO choose Restore, select that backup, and enter the same backup password. Everything stays offline and encrypted — there is no cloud step.
